How to Bill Blood Glucose Monitors:

(Bayer+Abott), (Bayer), (Abott), (Telus Page 49 - All Meters' Pseudo Dins/Pins)
Step Instructions for Bayer
1 Add BA (Bayer Meter Reimbursement) in patient's Third Party
2 Relationship: Last Name
3 Carrier ID: 80
4 Group: 500000
5 ID: Last 10 Digits of serial number + 01 (no dashes/spaces)
6 Save. and Then bill normally, using only BA, and don't forget to use the right DIN/PIN.
Step Instructions for Abbott
1 Add A3 (Abbott Reimbursement) in patient's Third Party
2 Relationship: Cardholder
3 Carrier ID: 80
4 Group: 330000
5 ID: Last 8 Digits of serial number (no dashes/spaces)
6 Save. and Then bill normally, using only A3, and don't forget to use the right DIN/PIN.
